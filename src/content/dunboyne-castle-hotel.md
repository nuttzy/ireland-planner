# Dunboyne Castle Hotel  
*Dunboyne, County Meath – Greater Dublin Area*

## A Brief History  
- The original **Dunboyne Castle** was built around **1764** for **Theobald Jones**, replacing an earlier Jacobean house on the same estate.  
- Designed in the **Georgian style**, it remained a private residence through several families before being sold in the 20th century.  
- The property underwent an extensive restoration and expansion, reopening in **2006** as the **Dunboyne Castle Hotel & Spa**—combining period architecture with a modern glass-and-stone wing.  
- The hotel stands on **21 acres of parkland**, only 25 minutes from central Dublin, offering a refined yet relaxed countryside base. ([dunboynecastlehotel.com](https://www.dunboynecastlehotel.com/))  

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Lawn & Façade** — Best light late afternoon; the cream Georgian exterior glows warmly against blue skies.  
- **Main Entrance Portico** — Classic columns and symmetry ideal for portraits.  
- **Spa Garden** — A quiet spot behind the modern wing with ornamental grasses and evening sun.  
- **Terrace Balcony** — Overlooks the lawns and tree line—lovely at golden hour.  
- **Night Exterior** — Subtle up-lighting accentuates the Georgian windows and cornices; wide shot from the circular drive works beautifully.

## Surrounding Area & Highlights  
- **Dublin City** — Quick drive or train ride for museums, Trinity College, Temple Bar, and the River Liffey.  
- **Trim Castle** — Ireland’s largest Norman castle, about 30 min west.  
- **Hill of Tara** — Ancient ceremonial site and panoramic viewpoint, 20 min away.  
- **County Meath countryside** — Rolling fields, horse farms, and quiet villages worth a short detour.

## Practical Notes for Your Stay  
- Address: Maynooth Road, Dunboyne, Co. Meath A86 PW63.  
- Large on-site spa—reserve appointments early if you’d like treatments.  
- The restaurant, *The Ivy*, has excellent seasonal menus emphasizing Irish produce.  
- Ideal final-night stop before departure from Dublin Airport (25 min).
