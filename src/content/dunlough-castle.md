# Dunlough Castle (Three Castles Head)  
*Mizen Peninsula, County Cork – Atlantic Coast*

## Overview
Perched on a knife-edge ridge at **Three Castles Head**, **Dunlough Castle** is one of Ireland’s most dramatic ruins: **three stone towers** tied together by **curtain walls** that span a narrow isthmus between the Atlantic cliffs and an inland lake. It’s remote, windy, and utterly unforgettable.

## History & Background
- Traditionally dated to **c. 1207**, associated with the **O’Mahony** chieftains who controlled sea routes and fisheries along West Cork.  
- The castle’s unique plan exploited the terrain: walls formed a barrier across the ridge, with towers anchoring the defense.  
- Abandoned by the early modern period; its isolation preserved the ruin in a near-mythic landscape.

## Must-See Highlights
- **Triple Towers & Curtain Wall** — Walk the ridge to feel how the walls barricade the pass.  
- **Lough Dunlough** — Dark lake immediately inland; mirror reflections on calm days.  
- **Cliff Edge Vistas** — Atlantic breakers hundreds of feet below.  
- **Old Field Walls & Gate Stiles** — Rustic details on the approach that frame the view.

## Photo Spots
- **Ridge Approach** — Classic shot of the three towers in a row.  
- **Lake Shore** — Towers reflected in Lough Dunlough (early morning is best).  
- **Clifftop Lookout** — Wide angle showing walls, lake, and ocean in one frame.  
- **Blue Hour** — Moody silhouettes against the western sky (bring a headlamp).

## Visitor Notes
- **Access:** Via a **private farm track** on Three Castles Head. There’s typically an **honesty box** at the parking area—bring cash and respect signage.  
- **Walk:** ~30–40 minutes each way over **uneven, sometimes boggy** ground; sturdy footwear required.  
- **Safety:** Exposed cliffs with sudden gusts—keep well back from edges, especially in wind or fog.  
- **Facilities:** None on site. Nearest services in **Goleen** or **Crookhaven**.  
- **Weather:** This is the Atlantic—conditions change fast. A waterproof and layers are wise.
