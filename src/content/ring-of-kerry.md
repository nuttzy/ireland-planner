# Ring of Kerry  
*County Kerry – Iveragh Peninsula*

## Overview  
The **Ring of Kerry** is Ireland’s most celebrated scenic route—a **179 km (111 mi)** loop through mountains, lakes, and Atlantic coastline around the Iveragh Peninsula. The road passes through charming villages, rugged passes, beaches, and viewpoints that showcase the best of Ireland’s wild southwest.

## History & Background  
- The route follows ancient roads that once linked **Iron Age forts, monastic sites, and coastal settlements**.  
- Popularized in the **19th century** as part of the “Grand Tour of Ireland,” it remains the definitive scenic drive.  
- Today it’s part of the **Wild Atlantic Way**, connecting Kerry’s natural and cultural highlights.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Killarney National Park** — Lakes, waterfalls, and Muckross House estate.  
- **Ladies View** — Famous panorama across the Lakes of Killarney.  
- **Torc Waterfall** — A short walk through forested foothills.  
- **Sneem Village** — Bright houses, river bridges, and good café stops.  
- **Staigue Fort** — Iron Age ring fort above the sea, free to explore.  
- **Waterville & Caherdaniel** — Coastal villages with beaches and stone forts.  
- **Skellig Ring Extension** — Optional detour with views to the **Skellig Islands** (Star Wars filming site).

## Photo Spots  
- **Ladies View** — The classic Killarney panorama.  
- **Ballinskelligs Bay** — Blue-green water and distant islands.  
- **Moll’s Gap** — Mountain curves with sweeping vistas.  
- **Ross Castle (Killarney)** — Lakeshore castle reflections at sunrise.  
- **Sneem River Bridge** — Bright village colors and water reflections.

## Visitor Notes  
- **Length:** ~179 km loop (allow a full day).  
- **Direction:** drive **counterclockwise** (anticlockwise) to avoid tour-bus traffic jams.  
- **Starting point:** Killarney or Kenmare are most common.  
- **Duration:** 6–8 hours with stops; 2–3 hours minimum for highlights only.  
- **Best time:** early morning start for quieter roads and softer light.  
- Plenty of cafés, restrooms, and scenic pull-offs along the route.
