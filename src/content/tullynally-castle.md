# Tullynally Castle  
*Castlepollard, County Westmeath – North Midlands of Ireland*

## Overview  
Set amid rolling parkland near Lough Derravaragh, **Tullynally Castle** has been home to the **Pakenham family**—the **Earls of Longford**—for more than **350 years**. Its sprawling Gothic Revival façade hides centuries of additions and stories, surrounded by woodlands, lakes, and some of the most peaceful castle gardens in Ireland.

## History & Background  
- The Pakenhams first acquired Tullynally in the **17th century**, when Sir Henry Pakenham received the estate following the Cromwellian settlement.  
- The original fortified house was transformed into a vast **Gothic Revival castle** between **1800–1820** under **Thomas Pakenham**, 2nd Earl of Longford.  
- Features over **120 rooms**, making it one of the largest inhabited private castles in Ireland.  
- The family has maintained residence ever since, preserving a unique continuity of heritage and hospitality.  
- The gardens, open to the public, blend **Victorian pleasure grounds** with exotic plantings inspired by travels to China and Tibet.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Castle Exterior** — Romantic Gothic towers, battlements, and long façade.  
- **Courtyard & Stable Yard** — Charming setting with a tearoom and ticket office.  
- **Walled Gardens** — Flower borders, ornamental lakes, and fountains.  
- **Woodland Walks** — Trails leading to follies, grottos, and old stone bridges.  
- **Chinese Garden & Grotto** — Unusual Oriental-inspired section tucked in the parklands.  
- **Victorian Kitchen Garden** — Reconstructed with herbs and espaliered fruit trees.

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Lawn** — Sweeping view of the castle framed by old oaks.  
- **Upper Terrace** — Overlooks the parkland and Lough Derravaragh valley.  
- **Chinese Garden Pagoda** — Colorful contrast amid greenery.  
- **Lake Path** — Calm reflections in the estate’s ornamental ponds.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Tullynally Castle Gardens, Castlepollard, Co. Westmeath, N91 HV58.  
- **Open to visitors** seasonally (usually April–October).  
- Castle interiors may open for guided tours on select dates; gardens open regularly.  
- Facilities: tea room, gift shop, restrooms, and ample parking.  
- Allow **1.5–2 hours** for gardens and walks; more for tours.  
- Family- and dog-friendly (leads required).
