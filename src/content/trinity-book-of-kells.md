# Trinity College – Book of Kells Experience  
*Dublin City Centre*

## Overview  
At the heart of **Trinity College Dublin**, the **Book of Kells Experience** celebrates Ireland’s greatest cultural treasure: the illuminated Gospel manuscript created by monks over 1,200 years ago. The recently refurbished exhibition combines interactive storytelling with the unforgettable **Long Room** of the Old Library—one of Europe’s most photogenic interiors.

## History & Background  
- The **Book of Kells** was produced around **800 AD**, likely on the island of Iona and later brought to Kells, County Meath, for safekeeping.  
- It contains the four Gospels of the New Testament, written in Latin and lavishly decorated with intricate Celtic knotwork and Christian symbolism.  
- The manuscript has been housed at **Trinity College** since the 17th century and is now preserved under controlled conditions in the Old Library.  
- In **2023**, Trinity opened a reimagined **Book of Kells Experience**, featuring digital projections, new interpretation galleries, and improved visitor flow.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Book of Kells Display** — View two volumes, with one open to decorated pages that rotate regularly.  
- **The Long Room** — A two-storey barrel-vaulted library lined with 200,000 of Trinity’s oldest books and marble busts.  
- **Interactive Galleries** — Multimedia panels explaining how the manuscript was made and illuminated.  
- **Campus Grounds** — Classical architecture, the Campanile, and cobbled squares.

## Photo Spots  
- **The Long Room** — Iconic shot under the central arch (photography currently allowed in the new experience area; check policy).  
- **Trinity Front Gate** — Framed by railings and the Campanile in background.  
- **College Green View** — Wide exterior of the 18th-century library façade.  
- **Night Illumination** — Campus softly lit after sunset.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Trinity College Dublin, College Green, Dublin 2, D02 PN40.  
- Tickets required; **advance booking online recommended** (often sells out).  
- Typical visit: **45–60 minutes** for the exhibition and library.  
- Located a short walk from Dublin Castle, Grafton Street, and Temple Bar.  
- Accessibility: fully accessible with ramps and lifts.
