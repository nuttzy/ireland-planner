# Knappogue Castle  
*Quin, County Clare – near Ennis and Bunratty*

## Overview  
Built by a branch of the **O’Brien clan** in the 15th century, **Knappogue Castle** is a classic example of an Irish tower house — strong, elegant, and steeped in clan history. Though restored for modern use, it retains much of its medieval character and charm, surrounded by walled gardens and peaceful countryside.

## History & Background  
- Constructed around **1467** by **Seán MacConmara (McNamara)**, a chieftain allied to the O’Briens of Thomond.  
- Later became an O’Brien stronghold, serving as a regional power base through centuries of shifting alliances.  
- Captured and recaptured multiple times during the **Cromwellian wars** and later restored by **Sir John MacNamara** in the 17th century.  
- Purchased in **1800** by the **Scott family**, who expanded it into a country house.  
- Extensively **restored in the 1960s** by the **Andrews family of Houston, Texas**, before being entrusted to **Shannon Development**; now operated by the **Office of Public Works (OPW)**.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Tower House Interior** — Restored great hall, stone stairways, and period furnishings.  
- **Walled Garden** — Beautifully kept with geometric beds and heritage roses.  
- **Banquet Experience** — Traditional music, mead toasts, and storytelling dinners (seasonal).  
- **Courtyard & Walkways** — Classic stonework with ivy and rural charm.  
- **Clan O’Brien Connection** — Learn how this castle ties to the lineage that ruled Thomond from Bunratty and beyond.

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Drive** — Castle façade framed by gardens.  
- **Walled Garden** — Bright florals and stone walls in symmetry.  
- **Courtyard Archway** — Great perspective for depth shots.  
- **Twilight Exterior** — Soft evening glow on limestone.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Knappogue Castle & Walled Garden, Quin, Co. Clare, V95 KW90.  
- Managed by **Shannon Heritage / OPW**.  
- **Open seasonally**, usually April–October.  
- Admission includes castle tour and garden access; banquet events require advance booking.  
- Facilities: café (seasonal), restrooms, and parking.  
- Allow **1–1.5 hours** for tour and garden visit.  
- Located about **15 minutes east of Ennis**, or **25 minutes from Bunratty**.
