# Cahir Castle  
*Cahir, County Tipperary – River Suir*

## Overview  
Rising from a rocky island in the River Suir, **Cahir Castle** is one of Ireland’s largest and best-preserved medieval fortresses. Its curtain walls, towers, and moated keep give a vivid sense of what a working Norman stronghold looked like. Easy access and cinematic appeal make it a favorite filming location and visitor stop.

## History & Background  
- Begun around **1142** by **Conor O’Brien, Prince of Thomond**, and rebuilt in the **13th century** by the **Butler family**.  
- Expanded and strengthened across the 14th–16th centuries; served as a key Butler stronghold controlling access along the Suir Valley.  
- Withstood multiple sieges; famously fell to **Lord Essex** in **1599** after heavy cannon fire—the scars of which still mark the walls.  
- Restored by the **Office of Public Works (OPW)** in the 20th century; now one of Ireland’s flagship heritage sites.  
- Featured in films including *Excalibur (1981)*, *The Last Duel (2021)*, and *The Tudors* TV series.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Great Keep & Courtyard** — Inner ward with towers, cannonballs, and intact battlements.  
- **River Walk & Weir** — Lovely view of the castle mirrored in the Suir.  
- **Portcullis Gate** — Fully functioning drawbridge and gatehouse.  
- **Exhibition Rooms** — Displays on the Butlers and the castle’s sieges.  
- **St Paul’s Church (nearby)** — Atmospheric ruin across the bridge.

## Photo Spots  
- **Main Bridge View** — Signature angle of the castle framed by river and trees.  
- **North Bank Park** — Wide-angle panorama showing the full complex.  
- **Inner Courtyard** — Arches, stairways, and defensive detail.  
- **Evening Light** — Warm reflections on the water for postcard shots.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Castle St, Cahir, Co. Tipperary E21 C409.  
- Managed by **Heritage Ireland**; open year-round (tickets on-site or online).  
- Allow **1–1.5 hours** for a full visit.  
- Facilities: visitor centre, restrooms, and small gift shop; cafés nearby in town.  
- Limited parking directly outside; larger car park across the bridge.
