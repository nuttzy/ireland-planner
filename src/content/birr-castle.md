# Birr Castle Demesne  
*Birr, County Offaly – Midlands of Ireland*

## Overview  
A living estate that blends **medieval roots, Georgian architecture, and scientific innovation**, **Birr Castle Demesne** has been home to the Parsons family—the **Earls of Rosse**—for over 400 years. The estate’s parklands, science museum, and world-famous **Great Telescope** make it one of Ireland’s most unique attractions.

## History & Background  
- The original fortress on the site dates to the **12th century**; the current castle incorporates those medieval foundations.  
- Seat of the **Parsons family** since **1620**, notable for their achievements in astronomy, engineering, and photography.  
- The **3rd Earl of Rosse**, **William Parsons**, constructed the **“Leviathan of Parsonstown”** in **1845**—a massive reflecting telescope that remained the largest in the world for over 70 years.  
- Today, the castle remains a private residence, but its **gardens, telescope, and science centre** are open to visitors.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **The Great Telescope (Leviathan of Parsonstown)** — A 1.8-meter (72-inch) reflecting telescope, fully restored, set between giant masonry walls.  
- **Birr Castle Gardens** — 120 acres of landscaped grounds, formal gardens, and rare trees.  
- **Science Centre** — Exhibits on the family’s astronomical and engineering legacy.  
- **River Camcor Waterfalls** — Picturesque cascade within the grounds.  
- **Castle Exterior** — Gothic Revival façade overlooking the park (interior remains private).

## Photo Spots  
- **Telescope Structure** — Iconic shot with the twin masonry walls framing the reflector.  
- **Formal Garden** — Symmetry and color in spring and summer.  
- **Castle Bridge** — View across the lake to the castle façade.  
- **Tree Walks** — Sun-dappled woodland trails and heritage trees.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Rosse Row, Birr, County Offaly, R42 VO27.  
- **Open daily** year-round for gardens and telescope; check website for winter hours.  
- Tickets: available online or at the gate.  
- Typical visit: **1.5–2 hours** for gardens and museum.  
- Café, restrooms, playground, and shop on site.  
- The castle interior is **not open** to the public.
