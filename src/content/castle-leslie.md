# Castle Leslie Estate  
*Glaslough, County Monaghan – Ulster heartlands*

## Overview  
Set beside a glittering lake in the rolling Monaghan countryside, **Castle Leslie Estate** is one of Ireland’s last great family-owned estates. The **Leslie family** has lived here since the 1660s, and today the castle operates as a boutique hotel known for its romantic atmosphere, equestrian traditions, and genuine old-world charm.

## History & Background  
- The Leslies settled in Glaslough during the **17th century**, and the current house was completed in **1871** in a **Scottish Baronial** style.  
- Designed by architect **Sir Charles Lanyon**, whose work also includes parts of Queen’s University Belfast.  
- Hosted countless notable visitors over the centuries, from **Winston Churchill** to **W.B. Yeats**.  
- In 2002, the castle gained worldwide fame when **Sir Paul McCartney** and **Heather Mills** married here.  
- Still owned and managed by the Leslie family, maintaining a blend of heritage and modern luxury.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Lakeside Castle** — Stone turrets reflected in Glaslough Lake; interior open to hotel guests and guided tours.  
- **Victorian Conservatory** — Glasshouse dining space with views across the water.  
- **Gardens & Woodland Trails** — Lakeside paths, bridges, and walled gardens.  
- **Equestrian Centre** — One of Ireland’s premier riding schools with scenic trails through the estate.  
- **Church of Ireland at Glaslough** — Family chapel with Leslie memorials and stained glass by Evie Hone.

## Photo Spots  
- **Lakeside Lawn** — Classic postcard view of the castle across the water.  
- **Bridge to the Boathouse** — Perfect framing for reflections.  
- **Front Drive** — Towered façade with gravel approach.  
- **Walled Garden** — Roses, fountains, and greenhouse light.  
- **Sunset Over Glaslough Lake** — One of the most peaceful scenes in Ulster.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Castle Leslie Estate, Glaslough, Co. Monaghan, H18 RT72.  
- **Open year-round**; accommodation, dining, and guided tours available.  
- Day visitors can explore gardens, lakeside paths, and the café.  
- Facilities: hotel, restaurants, spa, riding stables, and event venues.  
- Allow **1–2 hours** for a walk, or an overnight stay for the full experience.  
- Located about **90 minutes north of Dublin**; near the border with County Armagh.
