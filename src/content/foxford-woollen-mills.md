# Foxford Woollen Mills  
*Foxford, County Mayo – on the River Moy*

## Overview
Founded in **1892**, **Foxford Woollen Mills** is one of Ireland’s best-known working mills, famous for its soft lambswool and merino **blankets, throws, and scarves**. The visitor centre combines a short **mill tour**, design gallery, **café**, and a **large shop**—perfect for picking up an Irish sweater or travel-friendly textiles.

## Brief History
- Established by **Sister Agnes Morrogh-Bernard** to bring skilled employment to Foxford.
- Powered by the **River Moy**, the mill grew into a national brand supplying hospitals and the Irish army.
- Today it blends modern looms with traditional patterns (herringbone, windowpane, classic stripes).

## What to Do
- **Guided mill tour** (short, engaging): see warping, weaving, and finishing.
- **Shop**: blankets/throws, scarves, **Aran-style knitwear**, fine wool accessories, and Irish-made gifts.
- **Design floor**: seasonal colours and pattern stories.
- **Café**: solid soups, sandwiches, and bakes—nice lunch stop between Ballina and Castlebar.

## Buying Tips
- **Sweaters**: look for natural fibres (merino, lambswool, cashmere blends). Check knit tension and shoulder seams for durability.
- **Blankets/throws**: ask about fibre mix (pure wool vs. lambswool vs. merino). Foxford labels list composition and care.
- **Seconds/outlet rail**: often a small selection with tiny cosmetic flaws at a discount—worth a look.

## Visitor Notes
- Plan **60–90 minutes** (tour + browse + café).
- Easy parking on site; accessible restrooms.
- Combine with **Croagh Patrick/Westport** or a Moy riverside walk; Ballina is ~20 min.
