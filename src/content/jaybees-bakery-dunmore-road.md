# Jaybees Bakery – Dunmore Road  
*Near Dunmore East, County Waterford*

## What it is
A low-key **bakery + petrol station + local shop** run by an **Amish-Mennonite community**, famous for **brown bread**, **carrot cake**, and soft-serve **99s**—worth a dedicated stop on the Waterford→Dunmore East run.  
*Source notes:* community + petrol station details and treats are well-documented locally. [Background article] and [artisan roundup].

## What to try
- **Brown bread** (and other daily loaves)  
- **Carrot cake** (signature)  
- **Lemon drizzle / Madeira / brownies** (rotate)  
- A **99** from the shop window in summer

## Practical
- **Location:** R684 (Dunmore Road) toward **Dunmore East**  
- **When:** Daytime hours; go earlier for best selection  
- **Pair with:** **Tramore** beaches or **Dunmore East** harbour; grab bakes for a cliff walk picnic

## Why it made the list
Because sometimes the most memorable taste of Ireland is a slice of **warm brown bread** from a tiny roadside bakery with a story.
