# Maynooth Castle  
*Maynooth, County Kildare*

## Overview  
Standing at the entrance to the historic university town of Maynooth, **Maynooth Castle** was once one of the great medieval fortresses of Ireland. Though now in ruins, its massive gatehouse and partial keep walls testify to centuries of FitzGerald power in Leinster.

## History & Background  
- Built in the **early 13th century** by **Gerald FitzMaurice FitzGerald**, the first Baron of Offaly, establishing Maynooth as the seat of the powerful **FitzGerald earls of Kildare**.  
- Expanded over the following centuries into a sprawling complex dominating the town and surrounding plains.  
- The castle was **besieged and destroyed in 1535** during the rebellion of **Silken Thomas**, the 10th Earl of Kildare, against King Henry VIII.  
- Later partly rebuilt as a **manor house**, though it never regained its former grandeur.  
- The remains were conserved by the Office of Public Works (OPW) and now stand beside **Maynooth University**.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Great Gatehouse** — Striking stone façade and entrance archway still intact.  
- **Inner Courtyard** — Traces of halls and domestic buildings visible in the grassed enclosure.  
- **Interpretive Centre** — Small but informative exhibits on the FitzGerald dynasty.  
- **Town Park & College Connection** — Scenic setting right next to the university’s south campus.

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Gatehouse** — Best angle from the green along Main Street.  
- **Side Pathway** — Captures wall textures with ivy and partial towers.  
- **Interior Courtyard** — Low light for detail and stonework.  
- **Evening Golden Hour** — Warm tones on the western walls and surrounding park.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Main Street, Maynooth, Co. Kildare.  
- Admission: **Free**; seasonal guided tours (summer months) provided by the OPW.  
- Typical visit: **20–40 minutes**.  
- Parking available nearby in Maynooth town centre; cafés and shops within walking distance.
