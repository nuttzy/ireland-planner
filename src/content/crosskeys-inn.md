# The Crosskeys Inn  
*Toome, County Antrim – between Belfast and Derry*

## Overview  
Steeped in history and warm hospitality, **The Crosskeys Inn** is Ireland’s **oldest thatched pub**, dating back to the **1650s**. Once a coach stop along the old road between Belfast and Derry, it remains a timeless gathering place for travelers and locals alike — full of music, laughter, and the smell of turf smoke.

## Atmosphere & Dining  
- **Setting:** Whitewashed stone walls, low ceilings, peat fires, and flagstone floors create a cozy, authentic feel.  
- **Food:** Simple, hearty Irish dishes — think stews, roast beef sandwiches, and seafood chowder.  
- **Drinks:** Local whiskeys, craft ales, and a properly poured pint of Guinness.  
- **Music:** Traditional Irish sessions most weekends, with fiddles, bodhráns, and spontaneous sing-alongs.  

## History  
- Built in **1654**, The Crosskeys served as a rest stop for horse-drawn coaches and traders.  
- The thatched roof and stone structure have been carefully preserved and repaired using traditional methods.  
- Awarded numerous times as one of Ireland’s **best traditional pubs**, it has become a cultural landmark.  

## Photo Spots  
- **Front façade** – Classic white walls and thatched roof with the golden key sign.  
- **Interior hearth** – Firelight and instruments by the stone fireplace.  
- **Countryside views** – Rolling fields surrounding the crossroads.  

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: The Crosskeys Inn, Toome, Co. Antrim BT41 3QA.  
- **Open year-round**, with music sessions on most Friday and Saturday nights.  
- Food served daily; reservations recommended for evenings.  
- Parking available on-site.  
- Roughly **40 minutes from Belfast** and **1 hour from Derry** — ideal midpoint stop.
