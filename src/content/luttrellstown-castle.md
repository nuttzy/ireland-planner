# Luttrellstown Castle  
*Clonsilla / Castleknock, Dublin 15*

## Overview  
A grand castellated house on a **560-acre** private estate just west of Dublin, **Luttrellstown Castle** blends Gothic Revival drama with Georgian elegance. Today it’s an exclusive-hire venue known for high-profile weddings and events, yet the estate’s story stretches back to medieval Ireland.

## History & Background  
- The lands were associated with the **Luttrell** family for centuries; the estate name dates to the medieval period.  
- The current house evolved between the **15th–19th centuries**, with major Gothic/Georgian works completed by the early 1800s.  
- Later owners included **Luke White** (bookseller/financier) and the **Guinness** family in the 20th century; more recently the property was restored as a luxury events venue with **20 bedrooms**.  

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Garden Front & Terrace** — The signature façade with battlements and oriels.  
- **Estate Drives & Woodland** — Quiet lanes and mature trees framing the castle in long views.  
- **Golf Course Vistas** — Open sightlines to the Dublin Mountains from the resort side.  
- **Event Spaces** — The Van Stry Ballroom and Kentian Room (if visiting for a function).

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Lawn Axis** — Symmetrical shots of the garden façade (best late afternoon).  
- **Woodland Edge** — Castle peeking through trees for layered compositions.  
- **Golden Hour from the Drive** — Warm light on stone and ivy; wide angle recommended.  
- **Blue Hour** — Floodlit windows and reflected glow after sunset.

## Visitor Notes  
- Location: **Castleknock, Dublin 15 (D15 RH92)**.  
- The castle operates as an **exclusive-hire** venue; **interiors aren’t open** for general public tours.  
- You may still enjoy **external views** from nearby public roads/approaches; access to estate grounds is by reservation/event only.  
- If attending an event: confirm parking, entry gate, and photography guidelines in advance.
