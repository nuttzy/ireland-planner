# The Fields Pub  
*Kilkenny City – High Street*

## Overview  
Tucked into Kilkenny’s bustling High Street, **The Fields** is a traditional Irish pub with an easygoing local crowd and that “everyone knows your name” energy. A warm welcome, a creamy pint, and live trad tunes make it a perfect nightcap after exploring Kilkenny Castle or the Medieval Mile.

## What to Try  
- **A Proper Pint of Guinness** – Best enjoyed when Mary’s behind the bar.  
- **Local Music Nights** – Fiddle and guitar sessions most weekends.  
- **Toasted Sandwiches** – Late-night favorite with a pint.

## Atmosphere  
Wood-paneled bar, friendly chatter, occasional spontaneous sing-alongs. Don’t be surprised if you leave with a few new friends.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: The Fields Bar, 3 High St, Kilkenny, R95 XY29.  
- Open daily, afternoons until late.  
- No reservations needed.  
- Close to Kilkenny Castle and the Medieval Mile Museum.
