# Cork City – Butter Museum & English Market  
*Cork, County Cork – Ireland’s culinary heart*

## Overview  
Ireland’s second-largest city has long been known as its **food capital**, and nowhere is that more apparent than in the **Cork Butter Museum** and the **English Market**. Together they celebrate the city’s history of trade, craft, and taste — from centuries of butter exports to the modern artisan food scene.

---

## The Cork Butter Museum  
- Located in the historic **Shandon district**, the museum tells the story of how Cork became the **butter-trading hub of Europe** in the 18th and 19th centuries.  
- Exhibits include **antique churns**, the **Cork Butter Exchange**, and stories of the farmers who supplied the world’s largest butter market.  
- Learn how the Irish Dairy Board (now **Kerrygold**) grew from these humble beginnings.  
- Small but fascinating, it’s a quick stop that adds flavor to any Cork visit.

**Visitor Details:**  
- Address: Cork Butter Museum, Shandon, Cork T23 P8EA.  
- Open daily, with guided tours available.  
- Admission: modest fee; family-friendly.  
- Allow 30–45 minutes.

---

## The English Market  
- Established in **1788**, this covered market is the beating heart of Cork’s food culture.  
- Stalls brim with **local cheeses, fresh fish, breads, olives, chocolates, and artisan produce**.  
- Grab a **buttered corned beef sandwich**, or try **local smoked salmon** and **Clonakilty black pudding**.  
- It’s a meeting place for locals and chefs alike, blending old-world stalls with contemporary tastes.  

**Visitor Details:**  
- Address: English Market, Grand Parade, Cork City Centre.  
- Open Monday–Saturday, typically 8:00 AM–6:00 PM.  
- Entry is free; bring an appetite!  
- Excellent indoor stop on a rainy day.
