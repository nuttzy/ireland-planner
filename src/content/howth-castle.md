# Howth Castle  
*Howth, County Dublin – Overlooking Dublin Bay*

## Overview  
Nestled on the Howth Peninsula northeast of Dublin, **Howth Castle** has been the seat of the **St. Lawrence family** (later the **Earls of Howth**) for more than **800 years**, making it one of the longest continuously occupied private estates in Ireland. Surrounded by woodlands, rhododendron gardens, and sea views, the castle blends medieval roots with Georgian and Tudor Revival additions.

## History & Background  
- The first fortification at Howth dates to around **1177**, when the Norman knight **Almeric Tristram de St. Lawrence** received lands from the Anglo-Norman conquerors.  
- The early medieval tower evolved over centuries, with major **Tudor-style expansions in the 1500s–1700s**.  
- The **Great Hall**, with heraldic windows and carved oak beams, retains much of its historic charm.  
- In the 20th century, the estate became known for its **gardens, cookery school, and antique collections**.  
- Parts of the property now host **cafés, walking trails, and occasional guided tours** through the historic interiors.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Castle Exterior** — Mixture of medieval and Tudor Revival wings, ivy-clad and photogenic.  
- **Walled Gardens & Woodland Trails** — Walk to the **Rhododendron Gardens** and **Deer Park** for sweeping views of Dublin Bay.  
- **Howth Summit & Bailey Lighthouse** — A short drive or hike with jaw-dropping coastal panoramas.  
- **St. Mary’s Abbey Ruins** — Near the harbour; early monastic remains connected to the same family.  
- **Legend of Grace O’Malley** — The pirate queen once visited Howth and was famously refused dinner—her revenge reshaped Irish hospitality customs.

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Lawn** — Castle façade framed by old trees.  
- **Rhododendron Gardens** — Best in late spring.  
- **Deer Park Hotel Vista** — Elevated view of castle and bay.  
- **Harbour Approach** — Capture castle in the distance with sea light behind.

## Visitor Notes  
- Location: Howth, County Dublin (about 30 minutes from Dublin city by DART train).  
- The grounds are **open year-round** for walks and garden access.  
- Interior tours are **limited and seasonal**—check ahead for availability.  
- Café and food market often operate within the courtyard area.  
- Parking available near the main gate or Deer Park.  
- Ideal half-day stop combined with **Howth Cliff Walk** or **Harbour seafood restaurants**.
