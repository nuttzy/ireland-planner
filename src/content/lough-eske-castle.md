# Lough Eske Castle  
*Donegal Town, County Donegal – by the shores of Lough Eske*

## Overview  
**Lough Eske Castle** sits at the foot of the **Bluestack Mountains**, surrounded by forest and mirrored in the calm waters of **Lough Eske**. Though the current structure dates to the 19th century, the estate’s history reaches back to the **O’Donnell chieftains**, the rulers of medieval Donegal. Today it’s a luxury hotel, but one steeped in real heritage and natural beauty.

## History & Background  
- The site was originally part of the **O’Donnell clan’s** holdings — the same family that built nearby **Donegal Castle**.  
- The first manor house here was built around **1621**, likely by **Sir Basil Brooke**, after the O’Donnells lost their lands following the **Flight of the Earls** (1607).  
- The current **Elizabethan Revival-style castle** was built in **1861** by **Thomas Brooke**, incorporating parts of the earlier structure.  
- Destroyed by fire in **1939**, it lay in ruins for decades before being **restored in 2007** as a five-star hotel.  
- Despite its modern comforts, much of the 19th-century stonework and layout were carefully preserved.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Castle Exterior** — Elegant mix of towers, gables, and limestone detail reflected in the lake.  
- **Lough Eske Shoreline** — Peaceful trails and viewpoints ideal for photos.  
- **Bluestack Mountains** — Backdrop of forested slopes behind the castle.  
- **Hotel Interiors** — Grand staircase, stained glass, and restored fireplaces.  
- **Spa and Gardens** — Landscaped grounds with ancient trees and sculpture walks.

## Photo Spots  
- **Lakeside Lawn** — Classic view of the castle and its reflection.  
- **Driveway Approach** — Framed by beech and oak trees.  
- **Bluestack Vista** — Hills rising behind the castle at sunrise.  
- **Courtyard Fountain** — Charming symmetry in the central courtyard.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Lough Eske Castle, Lough Eske, Donegal Town, Co. Donegal F94 HX59.  
- **Open year-round**; day visitors welcome for dining, spa, or afternoon tea.  
- Grounds and lake walk accessible to guests; limited exterior access for non-residents.  
- Facilities: fine dining restaurant, bar, spa, walking trails, and car park.  
- Ideal for a **luxury stay** or scenic stop with lunch.  
- Located about **10 minutes east of Donegal Town**.
