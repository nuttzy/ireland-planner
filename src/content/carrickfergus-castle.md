# Carrickfergus Castle  
*Carrickfergus, County Antrim – Belfast Lough*

## Overview  
A formidable Norman fortress begun in the **late 12th century**, guarding the north shore of **Belfast Lough**. The castle dominated the region’s politics and trade for centuries and remains one of the best-preserved medieval structures in Northern Ireland. ## History & Background  
- Founded by **John de Courcy** after his 1177 invasion of Ulster; expanded over subsequent decades into a massive stone stronghold.  [oai_citation:2‡Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrickfergus_Castle?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
- Besieged at various times by **Scottish, Irish, English, and French** forces; continued in military use until the 20th century.  [oai_citation:3‡Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Carrickfergus_Castle?utm_source=chatgpt.com)  
- Later roles included **prison**, **magazine/armoury**, **WWI garrison**, and **WWII air-raid shelter**.  [oai_citation:4‡antrimcoastandglensaonb.ccght.org](https://antrimcoastandglensaonb.ccght.org/carrickfergus-castle/?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
