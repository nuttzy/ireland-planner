# Kilronan Castle  
*Ballyfarnon, County Roscommon*

## A Brief History  
- The original **Kilronan Castle**, also known as Castle Tenison, was built in the early **18th century** and later expanded in **the 1820s–1870s** by the Tenison family, who were local gentry connected to the King family of Boyle.  
- Architect **John Nash**, famed for Buckingham Palace renovations, is sometimes credited with influence on the Gothic Revival additions.  
- After a period of decline in the 20th century, the castle was restored and reopened as a luxury hotel in **2006**, blending historic stonework and modern amenities.  
- Overlooking **Lough Meelagh**, it remains one of Ireland’s most atmospheric castle hotels. ([kilronancastle.ie](https://www.kilronancastle.ie/))  

## Photo Spots
- **Lough Meelagh Shoreline** — Capture the castle’s reflection in still water at sunrise or sunset.  
- **Front Driveway & Portico** — The Gothic façade framed by old oaks; ideal for arrival photos.  
- **Inner Courtyard** — Soft light and symmetrical arches for portraits.  
- **Spa Terrace** — Panoramic views of Roscommon’s forested hills.  
- **Evening Exterior** — The castle’s lit towers mirrored in the lake — best taken after dusk.  

## Surrounding Area & Highlights  
- **Lough Key Forest Park** — 25 min drive; treetop walk, ruins, and cycling paths.  
- **Boyle Abbey & King House** — Excellent medieval and Georgian stops nearby.  
- **Arigna Mining Experience** — Fascinating tour through Ireland’s last working coal mine, just north of the castle.  
- **Roscommon countryside** — Lakes, peatlands, and classic Irish villages ideal for a slow scenic drive.  

## Practical Notes for Your Stay  
- Address: Ballyfarnon, Co. Roscommon, F52 R867.  
- Check the **spa availability** in advance — it’s a highlight of the stay.  
- Bring a tripod or steady surface for low-light lake shots.  
- Great morning walk: the lane toward Keadue village, with the lake to one side and stone walls lining the road.
