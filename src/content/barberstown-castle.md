# Barberstown Castle  
*Straffan, County Kildare – 25 km west of Dublin*

## Overview  
Dating back to the early **13th century**, **Barberstown Castle** blends medieval and Elizabethan architecture, its original tower house expanded over the centuries into a grand country manor. Now a boutique hotel, it’s one of the few Irish castles that combines genuine history with comfortable modern hospitality.

## History & Background  
- The earliest section of the castle was built around **1288**, likely as a defensive residence following the Norman invasion.  
- Owned successively by many prominent families including the **Barbers**, **Eustaces**, and **Huddlestons**, from whom it takes its name.  
- Expanded in the **17th–18th centuries**, adding elegant domestic wings alongside the medieval keep.  
- Restored in the **1970s** by musician **Eric Clapton**, who owned the castle for several years and hosted famous guests during its transformation into a hotel.  
- Today, it operates as a **4-star castle hotel**, combining historic stone interiors with gardens and fine dining.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Medieval Tower** — 13th-century core with vaulted stone rooms and spiral stairs.  
- **Manor House Wings** — Georgian additions blending seamlessly with the tower.  
- **Courtyard & Gardens** — Peaceful green spaces with climbing roses and fountains.  
- **Dining Rooms** — Locally sourced cuisine served in period surroundings.  
- **Historic Interiors** — Restored drawing rooms, fireplaces, and antiques.

## Photo Spots  
- **Main Gate & Tower** — Best front-facing composition of the old keep.  
- **Inner Courtyard** — Arched windows and ivy-covered stone.  
- **Garden Pathways** — Flowers and hedges framing the manor wings.  
- **Evening Light** — Castle façade warmly lit after sunset.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Straffan, Co. Kildare W23 CX40 (about 30 minutes west of Dublin).  
- Hotel guests can explore the grounds freely; non-guests may visit for dining or events.  
- Typical visit: **1–2 hours** for a meal and stroll, or overnight stay.  
- Facilities: full-service restaurant, bar, and private event spaces.  
- Ideal stop en route between Dublin and the Wicklow/Kildare countryside.
