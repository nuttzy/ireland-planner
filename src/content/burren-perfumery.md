# The Burren Perfumery  
*Carron (Carran), County Clare – in the heart of the Burren*

## Overview
A tiny, independent perfumery where **everything is made by hand on site** using natural/organic ingredients. Expect a calm shop, **herb gardens**, and a relaxed **tea room**—a perfect craft stop between the Burren and the Cliffs of Moher.
