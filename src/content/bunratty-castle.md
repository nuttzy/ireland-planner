# Bunratty Castle  
*Bunratty, County Clare – near Shannon and Limerick*

## Overview  
**Bunratty Castle** is Ireland’s most complete and authentically restored **15th-century fortress**. Dominating the Shannon estuary between Limerick and Ennis, it’s famous for its furnished interiors, evening banquets, and adjoining **Folk Park**, which recreates 19th-century Irish village life. Despite its tourist popularity, Bunratty remains one of the best places in Ireland to truly *feel* medieval architecture.

## History & Background  
- The site was originally fortified by Vikings around **970 AD**, later rebuilt as a Norman motte-and-bailey in the **13th century**.  
- The current stone castle was constructed in **1425** by the **MacNamara family**, who later handed it to the powerful **O’Brien clan**, Kings of Thomond.  
- The O’Briens made Bunratty their chief seat, entertaining poets, bishops, and earls in its great hall.  
- After centuries of war and decline, the castle was restored in the **1950s** by **Lord Gort** with help from the Office of Public Works.  
- Its rooms are now filled with **15th–17th century furnishings**, making it one of the few castles where visitors can explore intact medieval interiors.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Great Hall** — Vaulted chamber hung with tapestries, weapons, and portraits.  
- **Solar & Bedrooms** — Period furniture and four-poster beds.  
- **Battlements & Roof Walk** — 360° views of the Shannon estuary.  
- **Banquet Experience** — Medieval-style dining with traditional music and storytelling.  
- **Bunratty Folk Park** — Outdoor museum with cottages, shops, and farm animals, depicting Irish life from the 1800s to early 1900s.

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Lawn** — Full tower view with trees and flags.  
- **Courtyard Stair** — Atmospheric stone archways and iron gates.  
- **Battlements** — Wide-angle shot toward the Shannon estuary.  
- **Thatched Cottages in Folk Park** — Lovely rustic compositions.  
- **Twilight Exterior** — Castle floodlit against a deep blue sky.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Bunratty Castle & Folk Park, Bunratty, Co. Clare V95 TY02.  
- Managed by **Shannon Heritage**.  
- **Open daily**, with seasonal hours; banquet dinners by reservation.  
- Admission includes access to both the castle and the folk park.  
- Allow **2–3 hours** minimum; longer for the full experience.  
- Facilities: café, restrooms, picnic areas, and extensive parking.  
- Located just off the **N18**, 10 minutes from Shannon Airport.
