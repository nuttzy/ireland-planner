# National Gallery of Ireland  
*Dublin City Centre – Merrion Square*

## Overview
Ireland’s flagship art museum, spanning European masters and a world-class Irish collection (Yeats, Lavery, Orpen). Free entry to the permanent collection makes it an easy pair with Trinity and Grafton Street.

Picture: Workshop of Lucas Cranach the Elder, Judith with the Head of Holofernes, c 1550

## History & Background
- Founded **1854**; Main Building opened **1864**, later expanded (Millennium Wing, 2002; refurbishments in 2017).
- Holds 16th–20th century European works plus the definitive national collection of Irish art.

## Must-See Highlights
- **Caravaggio – *The Taking of Christ*** (attributed)  
- **Jack B. Yeats** galleries (color, movement, Irish life)  
- **Vermeer, Rembrandt, Goya, Monet** selections  
- **Irish Portrait Collection** in historic rooms

## Photo Spots
- **Millennium Wing atrium** (clean lines, natural light)  
- **Grand Stair & historic galleries** (mind signage for photo rules)  
- **Merrion Square façade** for a classic exterior shot

## Visitor Notes
- Address: Merrion Square West, Dublin 2.  
- Admission: **Free** (permanent collection); special exhibitions may be ticketed.  
- Typical visit: **45–90 minutes** (longer if doing a special show).  
- Café, shop, restrooms on site; cloakroom available.
