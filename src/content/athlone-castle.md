# Athlone Castle  
*Athlone, County Westmeath – on the River Shannon*

## Overview  
Standing guard over the River Shannon, **Athlone Castle** has defended the crossing between Leinster and Connacht for eight centuries. Once a medieval fortress and later a key garrison stronghold, it’s now home to one of Ireland’s most engaging **heritage and multimedia museums**.

## History & Background  
- Originally built in **1210** under the Anglo-Norman king **John de Gray**, on the orders of **King John of England**, to secure the strategic Shannon crossing.  
- Controlled by successive **Norman, Gaelic, and English forces**, reflecting centuries of frontier conflict.  
- Played a major role in the **Siege of Athlone (1691)** during the Williamite Wars, when the castle’s defenders fought to hold the bridge across the Shannon.  
- Extensively **rebuilt in the 19th century** as a British Army barracks.  
- Restored and opened as a modern museum in **2012**, combining original stonework with interactive exhibits and river views.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Great Courtyard** — Original limestone walls and cannon displays.  
- **Interactive Visitor Centre** — Exhibits on medieval life, the sieges, and 360° battle projections.  
- **The Keep Tower** — Restored interior with immersive storytelling.  
- **River Walk & Bridge View** — Classic photo spot over the Shannon with St. Peter’s and St. Paul’s Church in the distance.  
- **Family-friendly Activities** — Hands-on displays, armour try-ons, and kids’ discovery trails.

## Photo Spots  
- **Main Gate and Bridge** — Best composition with the river, castle walls, and bridge arches.  
- **Courtyard Cannons** — Foreground details against tower walls.  
- **Rooftop Lookout** — Panoramic view across Athlone town and the Shannon.  
- **Twilight from the South Bank** — Castle illuminated with reflections on calm water.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Athlone Castle Visitor Centre, Athlone, Co. Westmeath N37 A6D7.  
- **Open year-round** (seasonal hours; closed some Mondays in winter).  
- Typical visit: **60–90 minutes**.  
- Facilities: museum, restrooms, gift shop, and cafés nearby in Athlone town centre.  
- Parking: short-stay lot across the bridge; longer-term lots near the church.  
- Ideal stop between Galway and Dublin along the M6.
