# Ashford Castle  
*Cong, County Mayo – Lough Corrib*

## Overview  
Regal, romantic, and immaculately restored, **Ashford Castle** stands as one of Ireland’s most famous hotels. Its grey-stone towers rise over **Lough Corrib** at the edge of Cong village, surrounded by 350 acres of parkland and woodlands. Staying—or even visiting for tea—feels like stepping inside a fairy tale.

## History & Background  
- Originally built in **1228** by the **Anglo-Norman de Burgo (Burke) family** following their conquest of the region.  
- Passed through many hands, including the **Browne family** (17th–19th centuries), who expanded it into a country estate.  
- Purchased in **1852 by Sir Benjamin Lee Guinness**, the brewing magnate, who greatly enlarged the castle and added the Victorian extensions.  
- The Guinness family owned it until **1939**; it later became a luxury hotel and, after extensive restoration, re-opened in **2015** under the Red Carnation Hotel Collection.  
- Today it is a five-star property regularly listed among the best hotels in the world.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Castle Exterior & Gardens** — Turrets, bridges, and manicured lawns with sweeping lake views.  
- **Lough Corrib Shoreline** — Walking paths and boat tours departing from the castle’s pier.  
- **The Drawing Room & George V Dining Room** — Classic interiors and fine Irish cuisine.  
- **Falconry School** — Ireland’s oldest, offering private hawk-walk experiences.  
- **Cong Village** — Quaint nearby village famous as a filming location for *The Quiet Man* (1952).

## Photo Spots  
- **Stone Bridge over the River Cong** — Perfect postcard angle of the castle façade.  
- **Lakeshore Path** — Wide-angle views with reflections on calm days.  
- **Terrace Gardens** — Framed by hedges and fountains, ideal for sunrise or golden hour.  
- **Interior Details** — Chandeliers, staircases, and stained glass (for guests or guided visitors).

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Cong, County Mayo, F31 CA48.  
- Access: open to **overnight guests**; non-residents can pre-book afternoon tea, spa visits, or falconry experiences.  
- Allow 1–2 hours for a brief grounds stroll or much longer for dining/activities.  
- Dress code applies for certain dining rooms; reservations essential for non-guests.
