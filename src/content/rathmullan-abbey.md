# Rathmullan Abbey  
*Rathmullan, County Donegal – on Lough Swilly*

## Overview  
Overlooking the calm waters of **Lough Swilly**, the ruins of **Rathmullan Abbey** mark one of the most historic shorelines in Ireland. Founded as a Carmelite friary, it later became the scene of the **Flight of the Earls (1607)** — the departure of Ireland’s last great Gaelic chieftains into exile.

## History & Background  
- The abbey was founded around **1516** by **Eoghan Rua MacSweeney**, chieftain of the local clan who also held nearby **Doe Castle**.  
- Built for the **Carmelite order**, it replaced an earlier monastic settlement on the same site.  
- After **Henry VIII’s dissolution of the monasteries**, the abbey passed to secular hands and was fortified as a residence.  
- In **1607**, **Hugh O’Neill, Earl of Tyrone**, and **Rory O’Donnell, Earl of Tyrconnell**, departed from this very shore in the **Flight of the Earls**, marking the end of Gaelic Ireland’s dominance.  
- Today, only the abbey walls remain, but its seaside setting makes it one of the most evocative ruins in Donegal.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Abbey Ruins** — Surviving nave, transept, and cloister wall sections.  
- **Lough Swilly Shoreline** — Peaceful view across the water toward Inishowen.  
- **Interpretive Signage** — Summaries of Carmelite life and the Flight of the Earls.  
- **Beach Walk** — Lovely short stroll from the town to the ruins and pier.  
- **Rathmullan Heritage Centre** — Small exhibit (seasonal) with local artifacts and stories.

## Photo Spots  
- **Beachfront View** — Abbey framed by trees and shoreline.  
- **Inside the Nave** — Stone arches and ivy against the sky.  
- **Evening Light** — Golden-hour glow across Lough Swilly.  
- **Clifftop Path** — Wider panorama of the bay and ruins.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Rathmullan Abbey, Rathmullan, Co. Donegal F92 P863.  
- Free access year-round; ruins are ungated and signposted.  
- Parking nearby along the waterfront.  
- Facilities: cafés and restrooms in Rathmullan village.  
- Allow **30 minutes** for a relaxed visit and photos.  
- Combine easily with **Doe Castle** or **Fanad Head Lighthouse** for a scenic loop.
