# Ross Castle  
*Killarney, County Kerry – on the shores of Lough Leane*

## Overview  
Guarding the edge of **Lough Leane** in **Killarney National Park**, **Ross Castle** is a remarkably preserved **15th-century tower house** built by the **O’Donoghue chieftains**. With its stone ramparts mirrored in the lake and mountains in the background, it’s one of the most iconic sights in southwest Ireland.

## History & Background  
- Built in the late **1400s** by **Ross O’Donoghue**, a Gaelic lord of the Killarney region.  
- Served as the clan’s stronghold and symbol of power in the Kingdom of Desmond.  
- According to legend, **O’Donoghue Mór** still rides from the lake every seven years on a white horse to bless his descendants.  
- Fell to **Oliver Cromwell’s forces in 1652**, after a siege led by General Ludlow — one of the last Gaelic castles to surrender.  
- Restored by the **Office of Public Works (OPW)** and opened to the public; its interior is furnished to reflect 15th–17th century life.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Tower House Interior** — Restored great hall, sleeping chambers, and defensive stairs.  
- **Lough Leane Boat Tours** — Depart from the pier next to the castle.  
- **Lakeside Walk** — Short scenic loop with mountain and woodland views.  
- **Legends of O’Donoghue** — Interpretive signs explain local folklore.  
- **Nearby Killarney National Park** — Expansive trails, deer, and waterfalls.

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Lawn & Lakeshore** — Classic castle reflection shot.  
- **Boat Pier** — Framing the castle with the McGillycuddy Reeks behind.  
- **From the Water** — Short boat tours offer unique perspectives.  
- **Twilight & Early Morning** — Softest light for photography.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Ross Castle, Ross Road, Killarney, Co. Kerry V93 V304.  
- Managed by the **Office of Public Works (OPW)**.  
- **Open seasonally**, typically March–October, for guided tours.  
- Grounds open year-round; free to walk around.  
- Facilities: restrooms, ticket office, picnic area, nearby parking.  
- Allow **45–60 minutes** for the tour; longer for lakeside walks.  
- Boat trips available to **Innisfallen Island** (weather permitting).
