# Charleville Castle  
*Tullamore, County Offaly – Charleville Forest*

## Overview
Set deep within **Charleville Forest**, **Charleville Castle** is one of Ireland’s finest **Gothic Revival** houses—moody battlements, soaring towers, and lavish interiors designed to impress. Today it’s under ongoing restoration, with **volunteer-led tours** that reveal both grandeur and gritty conservation work.

## History & Background
- Commissioned by **Charles William Bury**, later **1st Earl of Charleville**, c. **1800–1812**.  
- Designed by architect **Francis Johnston** (also known for Dublin’s GPO), blending medieval romance with 19th-century showmanship.  
- Passed through periods of vacancy and decline; later saved by local advocates and a charitable trust now stewarding restoration.  
- Renowned for **paranormal lore**, adding to the castle’s dramatic reputation.

## Must-See Highlights
- **Grand Staircase & Hall** — Showpiece stone and timber detailing.  
- **Gallery & State Rooms** — Neo-Gothic plasterwork, portraits, and period features (as restored).  
- **Outer Keep & Towers** — Photogenic crenellations framed by forest.  
- **Charleville Forest** — Walking trails; look for ancient oaks and wildlife.

## Photo Spots
- **Front Approach** — Towered façade framed by avenue trees.  
- **Courtyard Angles** — Strong lines and shadow play under battlements.  
- **Stair Hall** — Atmospheric interior shots (check tour photography rules).  
- **Forest Edge at Dusk** — Castle silhouette against the treeline.

## Visitor Notes
- Location: **Charleville Demesne**, on the edge of **Tullamore**.  
- **Tours:** Typically **limited/seasonal**, run by volunteers; **book ahead** via the castle’s official channels or social pages.  
- Expect active **restoration areas**; some rooms may be closed or in flux.  
- Typical visit: **45–75 minutes** (longer for special events).  
- Facilities: basic on-site; cafés and parking in **Tullamore** town.
