# Ballynahinch Castle  
*Recess, County Galway – heart of Connemara*

## Overview  
Framed by mountains, forest, and river, **Ballynahinch Castle** is a historic estate turned luxury hotel set deep in **Connemara**. Once the seat of the **O’Flaherty chieftains** — and by marriage, the legendary pirate queen **Grace O’Malley** — it remains one of Ireland’s most enchanting castle settings.

## History & Background  
- The lands of Ballynahinch were held by the **O’Flaherty clan**, powerful lords of Connemara, from the medieval period onward.  
- The site’s name means *“house of the island”* (*Baile na hInse*), referencing the nearby island in Ballynahinch Lake.  
- In the late **1500s**, it became connected to **Grace O’Malley**, who married into the O’Flaherty family and controlled much of the Connemara coast.  
- The current manor house dates to the **17th–18th centuries**, incorporating older foundations.  
- Later owned by **Richard Martin**, known as “Humanity Dick,” founder of the Irish Society for the Prevention of Cruelty to Animals.  
- Restored in the **20th century**, it now serves as a luxury hotel, fishing lodge, and nature retreat.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Castle Exterior** — Elegant country manor façade beside the river and lake.  
- **Woodland & River Walks** — Trails through 700 acres of forest and lakeshore.  
- **Historic Interiors** — Oak paneling, fireplaces, and local artwork.  
- **Ballynahinch Lake** — Fishing boats and reflections of the Twelve Bens mountains.  
- **Grace O’Malley Legacy** — Local legends and interpretive displays celebrate her story.

## Photo Spots  
- **Main Lawn & Lakefront** — Iconic castle view with water reflections.  
- **Wooden Bridge** — Framing the castle over the Ballynahinch River.  
- **Forest Trails** — Mossy stone walls and ancient trees.  
- **Dusk Light** — Golden tones across the water and mountains.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Ballynahinch Castle, Recess, Co. Galway, H91 F4A7.  
- **Open year-round** as hotel and restaurant.  
- Day visitors welcome for meals, tea, or walks on the estate.  
- Facilities: hotel, dining, bar, walking trails, fishing, and parking.  
- Allow **1–2 hours** for exploring or longer for dining.  
- Located about **1 hour west of Galway City**, along the N59 scenic route.
