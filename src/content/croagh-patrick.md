# Croagh Patrick & Murrisk Abbey  
*Murrisk, County Mayo – overlooking Clew Bay*

## Overview  
Rising 764 m above Clew Bay, **Croagh Patrick** — known locally as **“The Reek”** — is both a sacred pilgrimage site and one of Ireland’s most scenic hikes. According to tradition, **St. Patrick fasted for 40 days** here in the 5th century. At its base lie the haunting ruins of **Murrisk Abbey**, linking the mountain’s natural majesty with centuries of faith and history.

## History & Background  
- Worship on this site dates back to **prehistoric times**, later Christianized when **St. Patrick** used the mountain as a place of penance around **441 AD**.  
- The climb has been a pilgrimage ever since; on **Reek Sunday** (the last Sunday in July), thousands still ascend barefoot in his honor.  
- **Murrisk Abbey**, founded in **1457** by the Augustinian friars under **Hugh O’Malley**, stands near the start of the path and served as a resting and prayer place for pilgrims.  
- A small **modern chapel**, built in **1905**, crowns the summit, continuing the unbroken spiritual link through the centuries.

## The Hike  
- **Distance:** ~7 km round-trip from the car park.  
- **Elevation gain:** ~750 m / 2,460 ft.  
- **Time:** 3–4 hours total for average hikers.  
- **Trail:** Steep, rocky path; sturdy shoes recommended (sections can be loose shale).  
- **Views:** Sweeping panoramas of **Clew Bay**, dotted with 365 islands, and the town of **Westport** below.  
- **Summit:** St. Patrick’s Oratory chapel and a wind-swept cairn of stones left by pilgrims.

## Murrisk Abbey Highlights  
- **Cloister Arches** — graceful Gothic stonework still standing.  
- **East Window** — frames Croagh Patrick perfectly in the background.  
- **Graveyard Walk** — tranquil and atmospheric, with 15th-century headstones.  
- **Information Panels** — detail the abbey’s role in the pilgrimage route.

## Photo Spots  
- **Base of the Reek:** Abbey ruins with the mountain behind.  
- **Mid-Trail:** Clew Bay spreading westward to the Atlantic.  
- **Summit Chapel:** Dramatic views at sunrise or late afternoon.  
- **Abbey Courtyard:** Stone arches against distant peaks.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Croagh Patrick Visitor Centre, Murrisk, Co. Mayo F28 RK11.  
- **Parking:** Pay and display at the visitor centre.  
- **Facilities:** café, restrooms, gift shop, and walking-stick rental.  
- Best hiking months: **April – September** (trail can be slick in winter).  
- **Allow 3–5 hours** for hike + abbey exploration.  
- Modest dress recommended on pilgrimage days.
