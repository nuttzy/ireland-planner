# Leap Castle  
*Near Roscrea, County Offaly*

## Overview
Perched on a low hill in central Ireland, **Leap Castle** has seen clan battles, murder, fire, and folklore—earning its title as **Ireland’s most haunted castle**. Despite its turbulent past, it remains a genuine family home, lovingly restored by its owner **Sean Ryan**, who welcomes visitors by prior arrangement.

## History & Background
- Believed to date from the **late 15th century**, built by the **O’Bannon clan** on land held by the **O’Carrolls of Ely**, one of the region’s most powerful families.  
- The name *“Leap”* (from “Leim Ui Bhanain”) refers to a legendary contest where O’Bannon brothers leapt from the rock to claim leadership.  
- Site of violent **O’Carroll feuds**, including the infamous “Bloody Chapel,” where one brother murdered another during mass.  
- Later passed to the **Darby family**, whose members wrote ghost stories inspired by their home.  
- Badly damaged by fire in **1922** during Ireland’s civil unrest, now carefully rebuilt and lived in once again.

## Must-See Highlights
- **The Bloody Chapel** — Top-floor chamber central to Leap’s darkest legends.  
- **Great Hall** — Restored with timber beams and personal artifacts.  
- **Dungeon (Oubliette)** — Pit where victims were allegedly dropped; now sealed.  
- **Grounds & Approach** — Atmospheric setting of trees and pastureland.  
- **Sean Ryan’s Tours** — Personal, informal storytelling by the musician-owner himself.

## Photo Spots
- **Front Approach** — Tower rising over the drive through trees.  
- **Side Angle with Wall** — Best to capture its rugged, weathered look.  
- **Sunset Silhouette** — Moody light perfect for a castle famed for ghosts.  
- **Interior (with permission)** — Rich stone texture and beams.

## Visitor Notes
- Location: Coolderry, Roscrea, County Offaly.  
- **Private residence** — visits by **appointment only** (contact ahead via the official page or social media).  
- Typical visit: **30–60 minutes** for tour and chat.  
- Modest admission; proceeds support ongoing restoration.  
- Facilities limited; nearest cafés and restrooms in Roscrea or Birr.
