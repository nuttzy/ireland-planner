# Kilkea Castle  
*Castledermot, County Kildare – near Athy and the Wicklow border*

## Overview  
Dating back to **1180**, **Kilkea Castle** is one of Ireland’s oldest continuously inhabited castles. Originally a Norman fortress, it became the ancestral seat of the **FitzGeralds, Earls of Kildare**, and carries centuries of legend — including tales of the “Wizard Earl.” Today it’s a restored hotel offering luxury stays amid ancient stone and lush countryside.

## History & Background  
- Built by **Sir Walter de Riddlesford** after the Norman invasion of Ireland (1170s).  
- Passed to the powerful **FitzGerald family** through marriage in the 13th century.  
- Served as the seat of the **Earls of Kildare** for nearly 700 years.  
- Associated with the legend of **Gerald, 11th Earl of Kildare**, known as the “Wizard Earl,” who was said to practice alchemy and to return to the castle every seven years on horseback.  
- Restored multiple times after periods of rebellion, confiscation, and neglect.  
- Extensively renovated in the **21st century** as a luxury resort, preserving its medieval architecture.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Castle Exterior & Courtyard** — Stone towers and ivy-clad walls beside manicured lawns.  
- **Great Hall & Staircases** — Original Gothic arches and decorative plasterwork.  
- **Woodland Walks** — Surrounding trails along the River Greese.  
- **Golf Course Views** — Wide, green landscapes with the castle as backdrop.  
- **Legend of the Wizard Earl** — Local folklore still tied to the castle’s mystique.

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Approach Drive** — Castle façade framed by trees.  
- **Inner Courtyard** — Arches and stairway details.  
- **Riverbank** — Reflections of the castle in calm water.  
- **Twilight Lights** — Castle illuminated at dusk.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Kilkea Castle, Castledermot, Co. Kildare R14 XE97.  
- **Open year-round** as hotel, restaurant, and event venue.  
- Day visitors welcome for afternoon tea or walks in the gardens.  
- Facilities: hotel, dining, golf, spa, and parking.  
- Located about **one hour from Dublin** via M9.  
- Allow **1–2 hours** for exterior exploration or a meal.
