# Trim Castle  
*Trim, County Meath – Boyne Valley*

## Overview  
Dominating the River Boyne in the heritage town of Trim, **Trim Castle** is Ireland’s largest Norman castle and one of its most photogenic ruins. With 20,000 m² of curtain walls, towers, and a striking three-storey keep, it tells the story of the Anglo-Norman foothold in Ireland and the strategic importance of the Boyne Valley.

## History & Background  
- Construction began in **1172** under **Hugh de Lacy**, the Anglo-Norman lord granted the Lordship of Meath by King Henry II.  
- The massive **cruciform keep** was completed around **1200**, surrounded by a protective ditch and curtain wall with several towers.  
- Over the centuries the castle was expanded, besieged, and repurposed; it later fell into decline but remained a landmark of Norman architecture.  
- In modern times, Trim Castle gained cinematic fame as a major filming location for **Mel Gibson’s *Braveheart* (1995)**.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **The Keep** — 20-sided stone structure unique in Ireland; climbable on guided tours.  
- **Curtain Walls & Towers** — Walk the perimeter to appreciate its defensive layout.  
- **River Walk** — Scenic path following the Boyne, giving excellent castle views.  
- **Town Gate & Ruins** — The nearby Yellow Steeple (St. Mary’s Abbey tower) completes the medieval skyline.

## Photo Spots  
- **Main Keep Approach** — Wide shot from the lawn with full view of walls and towers.  
- **Boyne Riverbank** — Capture the castle’s reflection in calm water at sunrise or sunset.  
- **South Curtain Wall** — Good perspective lines for depth shots.  
- **Yellow Steeple Viewpoint** — Frame both monuments in one composition from across the river.  
- **Night Illumination** — The keep is often floodlit—ideal for tripod night photography.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Castle Street, Trim, Co. Meath, C15 HR59.  
- Guided tours available seasonally; grounds open daily year-round.  
- Admission fee for interior tours; exterior and parkland access usually free.  
- Allow 1–1.5 hours for a full visit and short riverside walk.  
- Facilities: visitor centre, restrooms, café nearby in Trim town.
