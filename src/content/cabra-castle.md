# Cabra Castle  
*County Cavan – Kingscourt*

## A Brief History  
- The estate now known as Cabra Castle first dates to an older castle on the site of **Cormey Castle**, which was rebuilt in the early 19th century for the Foster family, but heavy costs forced sale to the Pratt family in 1813. ([cabracastle.com blog](https://www.cabracastle.com/blog/2020/03/20/the-history-of-cabra-castle/73-6/))
- The Pratts renamed it “Cabra Castle” around 1820. ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Cabra_Castle))  
- In 1964 the Brennan family purchased the property and converted it into a 22-bedroom hotel; later owners restored and expanded it into the luxury castle hotel you’ll stay in. ([cabracastle.com history](https://www.cabracastle.com/history))
- Set on approx. **100 acres of parkland and gardens** adjacent to the dawn-lit expanse of Dún a’ Rí Forest Park, Cabra Castle combines heritage architecture with modern hospitality.  ([cabracastle.com](https://www.cabracastle.com/))  

## Photo Spots
- **Front Approach** — Capture the full castle façade as you drive in from the main gates; best light mid-morning when the sun hits the front towers.  
- **Main Courtyard** — Symmetrical stone archways and ivy-clad walls make a great background for portraits.  
- **Rear Terrace & Gardens** — Gentle evening light falls across the lawns and overlooks Dún a’ Rí Forest Park.  
- **Woodland Path** — A short walk behind the castle leads into mature beech and oak trees—good depth shots in soft light.  
- **Gate Lodge** — The small stone lodge near the estate entrance offers a classic “framed-by-trees” angle back toward the castle.  
- **Night Exterior** — After dinner, step outside to photograph the castle lit from below—tripod or steady hand recommended.

## Surrounding Area & Highlights  
- **Dún a’ Rí Forest Park**: Just steps away, this forested sanctuary invites morning walks beneath old-growth trees, bluebell glades and the distant whisper of the Boundary River.  
- **County Cavan countryside**: Rolling drumlin hills, quiet lakes and farmland — a contrast to the coastal bustle of Ireland.  
- **Local points of interest**:  
   - Kingscourt village: quaint shops and cafés perfect for a relaxed afternoon stroll.  
   - The Cabra Estate demesne: explore historic features of the Pratt & Foster families, remnants of the old estate layout and garden follies.  
   - Nearby Lough Oughter or lakes in Cavan for a short detour if you want water views.

## Practical Notes for Your Stay  
- Arrive via Kingscourt on Carrickmacross Road (A82 EC64) and follow the sweeping drive into the estate.  
- Schedule: the castle hotel maintains modern comfort, but take a moment to wander the original corridors, stained-glass windows and battlements for that “castle living” feel.  
- Early riser? Consider a dawn walk through the parkland before breakfast — fewer other guests, soft light, and the castle in silhouette.
