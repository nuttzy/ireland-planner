# Carrigaholt Castle  
*Carrigaholt, County Clare – guarding the mouth of the River Shannon*

## Overview  
Perched above the tidal waters of the Shannon Estuary, **Carrigaholt Castle** is a striking **15th-century tower house** built by the **McMahon clan of West Clare**. Its name means “Rock of the Fleet,” a reminder of its maritime importance. Though a ruin today, it remains one of the most atmospheric castles on Ireland’s Atlantic coast.

## History & Background  
- Constructed around **1480** by **Conor Liath McMahon**, chief of the Corcabaskin branch of the McMahons.  
- The McMahons were powerful Gaelic lords who controlled much of the Loop Head Peninsula and exacted tolls from ships entering the Shannon.  
- The castle’s coastal position allowed it to serve as a **watchpoint and defensive fortress** at the mouth of the river.  
- In **1588**, after the wreck of the Spanish Armada, Carrigaholt briefly hosted survivors before being **besieged by Donagh O’Brien**, 4th Earl of Thomond, who captured and executed the McMahon defenders.  
- Later occupied by English garrisons; by the 18th century it had fallen into picturesque ruin.  
- The site today is maintained as a **National Monument** and freely accessible.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Five-Storey Tower House** — Strongly built with intact corner turrets and sea-facing windows.  
- **Estuary Viewpoint** — Sweeping panoramas over the Shannon mouth toward Kerry.  
- **Inner Courtyard & Seawall** — Defensive remains and traces of outbuildings.  
- **Grace O’Malley Connection** — The pirate queen is said to have visited here during her travels along the west coast.  
- **Loop Head Drive** — A stunning approach road with ocean cliffs, beaches, and birdlife.

## Photo Spots  
- **Pier & Beach Below the Castle** — Best full-height composition.  
- **Clifftop Road** — Castle against the estuary horizon.  
- **Evening Light** — Western sunset across the Shannon.  
- **Low Tide** — Exposed rocks and reflections below the keep.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Carrigaholt Castle, Carrigaholt, Co. Clare V15 EW65.  
- **Free access** year-round; open site beside the village pier.  
- Small parking area near the shore; short walk to the ruins.  
- Allow **20–30 minutes** for photos and exploration.  
- Combine with **Loop Head Lighthouse** or **Bridges of Ross** for a full coastal excursion.  
- The nearby village has cafés, pubs, and dolphin-watching boat tours.
