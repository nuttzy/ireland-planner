# Enniskillen Castle  
*Enniskillen, County Fermanagh – on Lough Erne*

## Overview  
Standing proudly on the banks of the **River Erne**, **Enniskillen Castle** is one of the best-preserved strongholds in Northern Ireland. Originally built by the Gaelic Maguire clan, it later became a key British garrison and now houses two fascinating museums that tell the story of Fermanagh and its people.

## History & Background  
- Constructed around **1428** by **Hugh Maguire**, chief of the Maguires, to guard a key crossing between Upper and Lower Lough Erne.  
- Served as the **Maguires’ main seat** until the early 17th century, when English forces seized and fortified it.  
- Extensively rebuilt in the **1600s**, including the distinctive **Watergate towers**, which still stand today.  
- Played a strategic role during the **Williamite Wars** and later as a British Army barracks.  
- Restored in the 20th century; now home to the **Fermanagh County Museum** and the **Inniskillings Museum**, chronicling local and regimental history.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Watergate Towers** — Twin circular turrets facing the river, among the most recognizable castle silhouettes in Ulster.  
- **Castle Keep & Courtyard** — Medieval walls and cannons overlooking the Erne.  
- **Fermanagh County Museum** — Archaeology, folklore, and local crafts.  
- **Inniskillings Museum** — Exhibits on the regiments raised here, with uniforms, medals, and wartime memorabilia.  
- **Lough Erne Views** — Riverside walkways offering great photo opportunities.

## Photo Spots  
- **Riverbank Path** — Castle reflection in the calm water.  
- **Watergate Towers** — Best shot from the opposite bank or bridge.  
- **Inner Courtyard** — Framed stone archways and displays.  
- **Dusk Lighting** — Warm floodlights on stone against deep blue evening sky.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Enniskillen Castle Museums, Enniskillen, Co. Fermanagh BT74 7HL.  
- Managed by **Fermanagh and Omagh District Council**.  
- **Open year-round**, with extended summer hours.  
- Admission covers both museums; family tickets available.  
- Allow **1.5–2 hours** to explore fully.  
- Facilities: café, restrooms, gift shop, and nearby parking.  
- Convenient walking distance from Enniskillen town centre.
