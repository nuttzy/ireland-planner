# Japanese Gardens – Irish National Stud  
*Tully, County Kildare – near Kildare Town*

## Overview  
Created between **1906 and 1910**, the **Japanese Gardens at Tully** are considered among the finest in Europe. Designed by Japanese master **Tassa Eida** and his son Minoru, they represent the **“Life of Man”** through carefully crafted landscapes — bridges, lanterns, streams, and pathways flowing through harmony and symbolism.

## History & Background  
- Commissioned by **Colonel William Hall-Walker** (later Lord Wavertree), founder of the **Irish National Stud**.  
- Tassa Eida, a landscape artist from Tokyo, and his son **Minoru Eida** designed and built the gardens by hand over four years.  
- Each section symbolizes stages of human existence: Birth, Childhood, Marriage, Ambition, Contentment, and Eternity.  
- The gardens remain a masterpiece of early 20th-century landscape design — blending **Japanese philosophy** with **Irish natural scenery**.  
- Now managed as part of the **Irish National Stud & Gardens**, which also includes the **St. Fiachra’s Garden**, celebrating Ireland’s monastic heritage.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Bridge of Life** — Represents the passage from youth to maturity.  
- **Hill of Ambition** — A climb with panoramic garden views.  
- **Well of Wisdom** — Symbolic source of knowledge and reflection.  
- **Tea House & Lanterns** — Authentic Japanese structures amid azaleas and cherry trees.  
- **St. Fiachra’s Garden** — Modern complement evoking Ireland’s natural spirituality.

## Photo Spots  
- **Red Bridge Over the Pond** — Classic composition with reflections.  
- **Tea House Terrace** — Framed by blossoms in spring.  
- **Hill Pathways** — Serene stone trails under trees.  
- **Zen Garden Area** — Peaceful close-ups of raked gravel and lanterns.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Irish National Stud & Gardens, Tully, Kildare Town, Co. Kildare R51 HR04.  
- **Open daily**, typically 10am–6pm (seasonal).  
- Admission covers both the Stud and Gardens.  
- Facilities: café, restrooms, shop, and guided tours.  
- Allow **1.5–2 hours** to explore both gardens and the stud farm.  
- Located about **50 minutes from Dublin** off the M7.
