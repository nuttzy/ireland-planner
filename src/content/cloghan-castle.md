# Cloghan Castle  
*Loughrea, County Galway – between Galway City and Portumna*

## Overview  
Rising above the fields of east Galway, **Cloghan Castle** is a restored **13th-century Norman tower house** with thick stone walls, sweeping countryside views, and deep historical roots. Once a medieval fortress, it’s now a private retreat available for exclusive rental — perfect for experiencing genuine castle life in modern comfort.

## History & Background  
- Built around **1239** by the **de Burgo (Burke)** family, one of the most powerful Norman dynasties in Connacht.  
- Constructed as part of a chain of fortifications controlling the fertile lands between Galway Bay and the River Shannon.  
- Passed through the **Burke, O’Madden, and O’Kelly** families over the centuries, reflecting shifting alliances between Norman and Gaelic lords.  
- Fell into ruin by the 19th century before being **lovingly restored in the 1990s** using traditional stonework.  
- Today the castle remains privately owned but is available for **exclusive self-catering rentals and weddings**, offering an authentic medieval atmosphere.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Tower House Exterior** — Classic Irish fortress silhouette with battlements and arrow slits.  
- **Interior Restoration** — Oak beams, stone fireplaces, and spiral stairs.  
- **Hilltop Views** — Wide panoramas over the Galway countryside.  
- **Surrounding Farmland** — Quiet lanes, meadows, and ancient stone walls.  
- **Historic Roots** — De Burgo heritage linking it to other great castles like Athenry and Loughrea.

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Approach Road** — Long driveway framing the tower.  
- **Castle Lawn** — Stone walls against rural backdrop.  
- **Battlements** — Views across rolling fields.  
- **Sunset Shots** — Castle glowing golden in evening light.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Cloghan Castle, Kilchreest, Loughrea, Co. Galway H62 TP38.  
- **Private property** — accessible to guests by booking only.  
- Short exterior viewing possible from nearby road; please respect private signage.  
- Facilities (for guests): 7 bedrooms, banquet hall, kitchens, and grounds.  
- Ideal overnight stop for groups or weddings.  
- Located about **40 minutes east of Galway City** and **20 minutes south of Athenry**.
