# Kilkenny Castle  
*Kilkenny City – River Nore*

## Overview  
A commanding stone fortress turned elegant manor, **Kilkenny Castle** dominates the city that grew around it. Once the seat of the powerful **Butler family**, it has evolved from 12th-century stronghold to 19th-century residence and remains one of Ireland’s most complete and visitor-friendly castles.

## History & Background  
- Originally built soon after **1172** by **Richard de Clare (“Strongbow”)**, marking Norman control of the region.  
- Purchased by **James Butler, 3rd Earl of Ormond**, in **1391**, beginning over 500 years of Butler family ownership.  
- Rebuilt and expanded in stages, with major 17th–19th-century remodeling transforming it into a stately home.  
- Sold to the people of Kilkenny in **1967** for £50 and subsequently restored by the **Office of Public Works (OPW)**.  
- Today the castle houses period rooms, an art gallery, and vast parkland open to the public.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **State Rooms Tour** — Restored Victorian drawing rooms, grand staircase, and portrait gallery.  
- **Rose Garden Terrace** — Formal design with a classic view of the castle façade.  
- **Long Gallery** — Ornate hammer-beam roof and 19th-century paintings.  
- **Kilkenny Castle Parklands** — 50 acres of lawns, woods, and riverside paths.  
- **National Design & Craft Gallery** (next door) — Irish contemporary craft exhibitions.

## Photo Spots  
- **The Parade** — Wide frontal view across the open lawn.  
- **Rose Garden** — Framed by hedges and fountains.  
- **River Walk** — Reflections of the castle on the Nore.  
- **Evening Light** — Western sun on limestone walls.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: The Parade, Kilkenny, R95 YRK1.  
- Guided and self-guided tours available; allow **1–1.5 hours** for interiors plus gardens.  
- Open year-round; café, restrooms, and picnic areas on site.  
- Parking and shops nearby in Kilkenny town centre.
