# Lismore Castle  
*Lismore, County Waterford – Blackwater Valley*

## Overview  
Rising dramatically above the River Blackwater, **Lismore Castle** blends centuries of Irish and English history in a single striking silhouette. Though the castle remains a private residence of the **Duke of Devonshire**, its **gardens and art gallery** are open to visitors, offering one of the most beautiful estate settings in the south of Ireland.

## History & Background  
- The site originated as a **monastic settlement** in the early Christian period and became the seat of a **bishopric** by the 12th century.  
- In **1185**, Prince **John (later King John)** built the first Norman fortification here; remnants of those walls survive in the lower structure.  
- The castle passed through the hands of **Sir Walter Raleigh**, then **Richard Boyle, the 1st Earl of Cork**, who expanded it into a grand residence in the 1620s.  
- It entered the **Cavendish family** (Dukes of Devonshire) in the 18th century, with major **Gothic Revival** remodeling by architect **William Cavendish** in the 19th century.  
- Today, while the main castle remains private, the **Upper and Lower Gardens** and **Lismore Castle Arts** contemporary gallery are open seasonally to the public.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Upper Gardens** — Terraced lawns, yew hedges, and panoramic views of the castle towers.  
- **Lower Gardens** — Woodland paths, modern sculptures, and the River Blackwater outlook.  
- **Lismore Castle Arts** — Rotating exhibitions of Irish and international contemporary art housed in restored stables and galleries.  
- **Lismore Cathedral & Town** — Nearby, the cathedral founded by St. Carthage and the charming stone town add context to the castle’s long story.

## Photo Spots  
- **Bridge Viewpoint (Riverside)** — Classic postcard angle with the arched bridge and castle above the Blackwater.  
- **Upper Garden Terrace** — Framed shots of towers and battlements across the rose beds.  
- **Townside Approach** — Capture the main gate façade with ivy and lanterns.  
- **Evening Light** — Late afternoon sun on the west-facing walls and turrets.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Lismore, County Waterford, Ireland.  
- Open seasonally (typically March–October) for gardens and gallery.  
- Allow 1½–2 hours for gardens and art exhibitions.  
- Interior of the castle is **not open** to the public, but the grounds and exterior views are excellent.  
- Facilities: café, gift shop, restrooms at entrance pavilion.
