# Doonagore Castle  
*Doolin, County Clare – overlooking the Atlantic Ocean*

## Overview  
Perched high on a hill above Doolin village, **Doonagore Castle** is a striking **16th-century round tower house** with sweeping views of the Atlantic and the Aran Islands. Though privately owned and not open to the public, it’s one of the most iconic silhouettes on Ireland’s Wild Atlantic Way.

## History & Background  
- Built around **1520** by **Teige MacTurlough MacCon O’Connor**, a member of a local Gaelic noble family.  
- Served as a **watchtower and fortified home**, part of the coastal defense network along Clare’s cliffs.  
- Captured in **1588** after the wreck of a Spanish Armada ship nearby — tradition says 170 survivors were executed here on English orders.  
- Fell into ruin by the 19th century and was **restored in the 1970s** as a private residence by an American architect.  
- Today it remains privately occupied but fully visible from nearby roads and walking trails.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Panoramic Setting** — Castle above Doolin with views of the Cliffs of Moher and Aran Islands.  
- **Roadside Viewpoint** — Excellent photo spot from the R478 between Doolin and Lisdoonvarna.  
- **Sunset Glow** — Evening light across the ocean makes the stone tower shimmer gold.  
- **Atlantic Wind & Clouds** — A quintessential Wild Atlantic Way experience.

## Photo Spots  
- **Doolin Coast Road** — Perfect composition of tower, fields, and sea.  
- **Near Doonagore Graveyard** — Slight elevation for full silhouette.  
- **At Sunset** — Tower framed by orange skies and green slopes.  
- **Stormy Days** — Dramatic sky contrasts beautifully with the stone walls.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Doonagore, Doolin, Co. Clare, V95 C9W9.  
- **Private property** – no entry beyond gate, but excellent roadside views.  
- Parking: small lay-by on the Doolin–Lisdoonvarna road.  
- Allow **10–15 minutes** for photos and panorama.  
- Combine with **Cliffs of Moher**, **Doolin Cave**, or **Lahinch** on a scenic drive.
