# McDermott’s Pub – Doolin  
*Doolin, County Clare – Ireland’s coastal music capital*

## Overview  
Set in the heart of the seaside village of **Doolin**, **McDermott’s Pub** is one of Ireland’s most beloved traditional music pubs. Alongside its cozy atmosphere and home-cooked fare, it’s renowned for serving some of the best **fish and chips** in County Clare — best enjoyed on the patio after a day exploring the nearby **Cliffs of Moher** or **Doonagore Castle**.

## Setting  
Doolin sits on Ireland’s **Wild Atlantic Way**, with colorful cottages, limestone cliffs, and views toward the **Aran Islands**. The area has become the **traditional music capital of Ireland**, where locals and travelers alike gather nightly for impromptu “trad” sessions of fiddles, bodhráns, and tin whistles.

## The Pub Experience  
- **Signature Dish:** Crispy local **fish and chips**, served with house tartar sauce and garden peas — often cited as some of the best in Clare.  
- **Music Sessions:** Regular traditional sessions with local musicians; spontaneous gatherings are common in the evenings.  
- **Atmosphere:** Warm, rustic, and communal, with a family-run charm that feels genuinely Irish.  
- **Outdoor Patio:** Relaxed seating perfect for a pint and meal in fine weather.  
- **Location:** Just off the main road through Doolin, a short walk from McGann’s Pub and Fitzpatrick’s Bar.

## Photo Spots  
- **From the Road to Doonagore Castle** — Iconic view of the round tower and Atlantic.  
- **Village Streets** — Bright cottages and pub signs under coastal skies.  
- **At Sunset** — Golden light over the fields and the Atlantic horizon.

## Nearby Attractions  
- **Doonagore Castle** — 16th-century tower house overlooking the sea.  
- **Cliffs of Moher** — Ireland’s most famous coastal cliffs, 10 minutes away.  
- **Doolin Cave** — Home to one of Europe’s longest free-hanging stalactites.  
- **Ferry to the Aran Islands** — Departing from Doolin Pier.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: McDermott’s Pub, Doolin, Co. Clare V95 Y2CN.  
- **Open daily** (hours vary seasonally).  
- Live music most nights in summer.  
- Seating: indoor and outdoor patio.  
- Parking available along the main road.  
- Expect friendly service and hearty local fare.
