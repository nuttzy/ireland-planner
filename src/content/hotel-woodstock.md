# Hotel Woodstock  
*Ennis, County Clare*

## A Brief History  
- **Hotel Woodstock** stands on what was once part of the **Woodstock Estate**, near the historic market town of Ennis.  
- The estate lands date back to the **18th century**, with the modern hotel built in **1999** and recently refurbished to capture a blend of country-house comfort and contemporary design.  
- Family-run and warmly Irish in atmosphere, it serves as a welcoming base for exploring Clare’s dramatic coast and limestone landscapes. ([hotelwoodstock.ie](https://www.hotelwoodstock.ie/))  

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Drive & Entrance** — The curved approach road frames the Georgian-inspired façade; morning or late-afternoon light works best.  
- **Golf Course Viewpoint** — Capture the castle-style architecture against rolling fairways and wooded hills beyond.  
- **Lobby Staircase** — Natural light from high windows makes an elegant portrait location.  
- **Ennis Town Centre** — Ten minutes away; colourful shopfronts and cobbled lanes for lively street photography.  
- **Evening Courtyard** — Softly lit terraces ideal for twilight shots with warm hotel lighting.

## Surrounding Area & Highlights  
- **The Burren** — A short drive north: lunar limestone pavements, wildflowers, and prehistoric tombs.  
- **Cliffs of Moher** — About 40 minutes west; Ireland’s most iconic sea cliffs.  
- **Ennis Friary** — 13th-century Franciscan site in the town centre with intricate carvings.  
- **Bunratty Castle & Folk Park** — Family-friendly heritage stop east of Ennis.  
- **Lahinch & Doolin** — Coastal villages famous for surf, pubs, and music.

## Practical Notes for Your Stay  
- Address: Shanaway Road, Ennis, Co. Clare V95 014.  
- Good option for laundry or supply top-ups midway through the trip.  
- The hotel’s restaurant often features local Clare seafood—worth reserving a table.  
- Morning walk suggestion: stroll a few holes of the Woodstock Golf Course trail behind the hotel.

**Why stay here?**  
Hotel Woodstock gives you a relaxed, modern base within reach of Ireland’s west-coast highlights. Friendly service, easy parking, and proximity to the Burren and Cliffs of Moher make it a practical and comfortable waypoint between castle stays.
