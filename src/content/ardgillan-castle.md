# Ardgillan Castle  
*Balbriggan, County Dublin – overlooking the Irish Sea*

## Overview  
Set on a hilltop with sweeping views of the **Irish Sea**, **Ardgillan Castle** is a beautifully preserved **Georgian country house** surrounded by 200 acres of parkland, rose gardens, and woodlands. Though called a “castle,” it’s really a grand 18th-century manor imbued with romantic charm and coastal air.

## History & Background  
- Built between **1738–1745** by **Rev. Robert Taylor**, whose descendants lived here for nearly 200 years.  
- The name *Ardgillan* comes from *Ard Choill* — “high wood” — describing its elevated, forested setting.  
- The **Taylor family** expanded the estate in the 19th century, adding glasshouses, a walled garden, and a watchtower for sea views.  
- Purchased by **Fingal County Council** in 1982 and restored as a public heritage site with guided tours, gardens, and tea rooms.  
- Famous locally for the **“Lady’s Stairs” legend**, said to involve a ghostly figure near the shore path.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Castle Interior Tours** — Restored period rooms, portraits, and furniture spanning Georgian to Victorian eras.  
- **Walled Gardens & Rose Garden** — Especially beautiful from late spring through autumn.  
- **Sea View Lawn** — Expansive green with panoramas toward Lambay Island.  
- **Tea Rooms & Courtyard Café** — Perfect stop after a walk.  
- **Woodland Trails** — Link to coastal paths and Balbriggan beach area.

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Terrace** — Classic view of the house framed by lawns and sky.  
- **South Garden** — Flowers with sea backdrop.  
- **Rose Garden Fountain** — Symmetrical photo op.  
- **Watchtower Lookout** — Wide-angle view of coast and estate.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Ardgillan Demesne, Balbriggan, Co. Dublin K32 0036.  
- Managed by **Fingal County Council**; open daily (free grounds access).  
- Guided tours available for a small fee; check seasonal hours.  
- Facilities: parking, café, playground, restrooms, picnic areas.  
- Allow **1.5–2 hours** for a relaxed visit or walk.  
- Dogs allowed on leads in parkland areas.
