# Malahide Castle  
*Malahide, County Dublin – North Coast*

## Overview  
One of Ireland’s oldest continuously inhabited castles, **Malahide Castle** offers an unusually complete look at aristocratic life through the centuries. Just north of Dublin, it sits within a 260-acre demesne of gardens, woodland, and walking trails—making it both a historic site and a relaxing day trip destination.

## History & Background  
- The estate originated with the **Talbot family**, who received the land from **King Henry II** around **1185**.  
- The Talbots lived here for nearly **800 years**, spanning Norman conquest, English rule, and Irish independence.  
- The castle evolved from a **13th-century fortress** into a **Tudor and later Georgian residence**, reflecting centuries of architectural layering.  
- In 1975, the final Talbot heir, **Rose Talbot**, sold the property to the Irish State; it now operates as a museum and event venue.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Guided Castle Tour** — Fully furnished rooms with portraits, tapestries, and Talbot family heirlooms.  
- **Great Hall** — Portraits by Sir Joshua Reynolds and other masters.  
- **Botanical Walled Garden** — Over 5,000 plant species curated by Lord Milo Talbot.  
- **Butterfly House & Fairy Trail** — Family-friendly diversions within the estate.  
- **Avoca Café & Store** — Modern Irish food and crafts in the courtyard.

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Lawn Axis** — Classic façade framed by mature trees.  
- **Walled Garden Walks** — Seasonal color and sculptural layout.  
- **Castle Interior Staircase** — Ornate woodwork and portraits (allowed on tour).  
- **Evening Light Across Parkland** — Golden hour on the west-facing stone walls.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Malahide Demesne, Malahide, Co. Dublin, K36 C432.  
- Located 30 minutes north of Dublin city centre (by DART train or car).  
- Open year-round with guided tours; last entry usually mid-afternoon.  
- Typical visit: **1–2 hours** for tour and gardens; more if walking the demesne.  
- Café, shop, restrooms, and picnic areas on site.
