# Slieve Russell Hotel Golf & Country Club

*Ballyconnell, County Cavan*

## Overview

- A large four-star country resort set on **300 acres**, including approximately **50 acres of lakes**.
- This is **not a historic castle**. Think polished Irish country resort rather than medieval atmosphere.
- The trade-off is that there is considerably more to do here than at many castle hotels: restaurants, bar, pool, spa, golf and estate walks.
- We are staying **two nights**, with breakfast included and dinner already paid for on at least one evening.
- This is a place where spending some time at the hotel itself is worthwhile rather than treating it merely as somewhere to sleep.

## The Resort

- The hotel has **224 rooms** surrounded by landscaped grounds, lakes and a championship golf course.
- The estate has welcomed guests since **1990**.
- The **PGA National Slieve Russell** is an 18-hole championship course built into the natural drumlin-and-lake landscape.
- The **Ciúin Spa** ("ciúin" means calm or quiet) offers treatments and a separate hydrotherapy/thermal area.
- The Country Club includes:
  - 20-metre swimming pool
  - Jacuzzi
  - Sauna
  - Steam room
  - Fitness room

## Worth Doing at the Hotel

### Cranaghan Walkway

- An approximately **2-mile walk** around the edge of the golf course.
- Good low-effort activity if we arrive with some time before dinner or want a walk after breakfast.
- Offers views across the surrounding Cavan drumlin countryside.
- Probably the easiest way to actually enjoy the estate without committing to golf or a spa appointment.

### Pool & Leisure Centre

- Hotel guests have access to the Country Club leisure facilities.
- The pool includes whirlpool and massage areas.
- **Swim caps are required** and can be purchased at the Country Club if needed.
- This is separate from the more elaborate Ciúin Spa hydrotherapy facilities.

### Ciúin Spa

- Full spa treatments are available if we want to turn part of the stay into a relaxation day.
- The Hydrotherapy Suite includes a steam room, sauna, jacuzzi, salt grotto, heated relaxation loungers, foot spas and specialty showers.
- **Pre-booking is required**, and weekend availability can be limited.
- Probably not something to count on spontaneously after arriving.

## Dinner

There are several very different choices, which is useful for a two-night stay.

### Conall Cearnach Restaurant

- The hotel's main and most formal restaurant.
- Focuses on seasonal produce and Irish ingredients.
- Dinner is normally served **6:00–9:15 PM**.
- Recent guests frequently single this restaurant out for both food and atmosphere.
- **Best choice for our included dinner**, assuming our package allows us to select the restaurant.
- Advance booking is strongly recommended.

### Summit Restaurant

- More casual than Conall Cearnach.
- Located at the golf clubhouse with views across the championship course.
- Serves an à-la-carte evening menu.
- A good candidate for our **second night** if we want an easy evening without repeating the formal restaurant.

### Setanta Restaurant

- Contemporary European-style dining.
- More polished than a pub but somewhat less traditional than Conall Cearnach.
- Opening is **seasonal**, so don't assume it will be operating during our stay.

### Kells Bar

- Relaxed hotel bar serving drinks, sandwiches and lighter food.
- No reservation required.
- Better for drinks or a lighter meal than for our primary dinner.
- Recent reviews often mention live music in the bar, particularly on busier evenings.

## Second-Night Dinner Options

We do **not** necessarily need to leave the resort.

**Easiest plan:** eat the included dinner at Conall Cearnach one night and try Summit the other. That gives us two different experiences without driving anywhere.

If we want to leave the resort for a more local evening, Ballyconnell is only a few minutes away:

- **The Anglers Rest** – traditional Irish pub/restaurant; steak and fish & chips are frequently praised. Probably the best option if we want a casual local pub dinner.
- **The Bridge** – small restaurant with Irish and Mediterranean influences, seafood, steak and tapas-style dishes; better suited to a slightly nicer night out.
- **Sophia's Ristorante** – Italian/European option if we want a complete break from Irish hotel food.

None of these needs its own POI unless we decide in advance that we specifically want to eat there.

## Visitor Notes

- **Check-in:** from 3:30 PM
- **Check-out:** 11:30 AM
- Complimentary parking is extensive.
- Breakfast is normally served **7:30–10:30 AM**.
- The hotel specifically asks guests to **reserve breakfast and dinner in advance**.
- If our included dinner doesn't already have a restaurant and time assigned, we should arrange that before the trip rather than waiting until check-in.
- The hotel can be busy with weddings, golf groups and families; recent reviews are generally very positive but occasionally mention slower service or crowded leisure facilities during busy periods.
- Classic rooms accommodate one or two guests with either double or twin beds. They are the hotel's more straightforward room category rather than a premium-view room.

## Why Stay?

Slieve Russell isn't the castle experience we originally expected here, but it looks like a **very solid replacement rather than a consolation prize**.

The attraction is the whole resort: landscaped grounds, lakes, an easy walking route, good leisure facilities and enough dining choices that a two-night stay should not feel repetitive.

For us, the best approach is probably to **use the hotel as part of the trip rather than merely our base**—explore County Cavan during the day, then come back early enough to walk the grounds, have dinner and enjoy the resort.
