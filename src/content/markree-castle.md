# Markree Castle  
*Collooney, County Sligo – Northwest Ireland*

## Overview  
Rising above the River Unshin, **Markree Castle** is one of Ireland’s grandest **Gothic Revival** country houses, now restored as a family-run hotel. Its blend of romantic architecture, scientific heritage, and modern hospitality makes it a highlight along the Wild Atlantic Way.

## History & Background  
- The original Markree estate was granted to the **Cooper family** in **1663** by King Charles II, and they have remained associated with it ever since.  
- The castle was largely rebuilt in **1802** in a dramatic Gothic style by **Francis Johnston**, architect of Charleville Castle and Dublin’s GPO.  
- In the 19th century, **Edward Joshua Cooper** established the **Markree Observatory** here — one of the most advanced in the world at the time.  
- In **1848**, astronomer **Andrew Graham** discovered the asteroid **(9) Metis** from Markree, the only asteroid ever discovered from Ireland.  
- Purchased and lovingly restored by the **Corscadden family** (who also own Cabra and Bellingham castles), reopening in 2017 as a luxury hotel.

## Must-See Highlights  
- **Castle Exterior** — Turrets, battlements, and sweeping lawns along the Unshin River.  
- **Grand Hall & Staircase** — Restored plasterwork, stained glass, and high Gothic arches.  
- **Dining Rooms** — Elegant vaulted ceilings and period décor.  
- **Chapel & Conservatory** — Restored for weddings and events.  
- **Observatory Site** — Ruins of the 19th-century telescope dome in the grounds.

## Photo Spots  
- **Front Approach Drive** — Iconic castle façade framed by trees.  
- **Riverbank Path** — Peaceful reflection shots along the Unshin.  
- **Main Staircase** — Ornate detailing under stained glass.  
- **Gardens at Sunset** — Castle glow with western light.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Markree Castle, Collooney, Co. Sligo F91 AE81.  
- **Hotel and event venue**; castle accessible via accommodation or dining booking.  
- Grounds open to guests; exterior views visible from nearby roads and trails.  
- Facilities: fine dining restaurant, bar, chapel, and gardens.  
- Typical visit: **overnight stay** or **1–2 hours** for lunch and a stroll.  
- About **15 minutes from Sligo town** and **2.5 hours from Dublin**.
