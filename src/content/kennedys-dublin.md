# Kennedy’s Pub  
*Dublin City Centre – Merrion Square*

## Overview  
A true Dublin landmark, **Kennedy’s of Westland Row** has been pouring pints since the 1850s. The pub is just steps from **Trinity College** and **Merrion Square**, making it a perfect stop for a casual meal and a bit of literary history — it was once frequented by **Oscar Wilde** when he lived nearby.

## What to Try  
- **Irish Stew & Brown Bread** – Tender lamb, carrots, and potatoes in a rich broth.  
- **Orchard Thieves Cider** – Crisp, lightly sweet, and perfectly refreshing.  
- **Seafood Chowder** or **Beef & Guinness Pie** – Local favorites if you’re hungry.

## Atmosphere  
Traditional wood-paneled interior with friendly bar staff and plenty of Dublin character. Popular with both locals and visiting students.

## Visitor Notes  
- Address: Kennedy’s Pub, 30–32 Westland Row, Dublin 2, D02 DP93.  
- Open daily, lunch through late night.  
- Near Trinity College and Merrion Square Park.  
- Easy walk from Pearse Street Station.
